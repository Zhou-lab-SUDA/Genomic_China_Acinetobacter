# Clade-prevalence sampling sensitivity

Annual and overall ESL2.3, ESL2.4 and ESL2.5 proportions were calculated under
four metadata-resampling schemes: 50% random downsampling, year balancing, city
balancing and combined city/year balancing. Each scheme contains 100 fixed-seed
replicates.

The resampling analysis addresses uneven numbers of genomes among years and
cities. The replicate intervals quantify how the observed temporal replacement
pattern varies within the available genome collection under each sampling
scheme.
