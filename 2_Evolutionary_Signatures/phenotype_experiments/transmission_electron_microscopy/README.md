# Transmission electron microscopy

Single colonies were grown overnight in LB at 37 degrees C, harvested by
low-speed centrifugation and washed with PBS. Cells were fixed in 2.5%
glutaraldehyde. A 10-microlitre aliquot was applied to a glow-discharged
continuous-carbon grid for 10 min, washed with distilled water, stained with
3% uranyl acetate for 3-4 min and imaged on a Hitachi HT7800 at 80-100 kV.

The protocol covers fixation, negative staining and image acquisition for the
representative micrographs.

Representative ESL2.4-KL2 and ESL2.5-KL3 micrographs are shown in [`../capsule_and_microscopy.png`](../capsule_and_microscopy.png).
