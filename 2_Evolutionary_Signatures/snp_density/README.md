# Lineage-associated SNP density

The genome was divided into 10-kb windows after RecHMM-based recombination
masking. Tables are provided for all annotated SNPs and for the nonsynonymous
subset.

The all-variant and protein-changing profiles are calculated independently for
ESL2.5 and non-ESL2.5 genomes. The highest ESL2.5 window is
3,897,000-3,897,999 bp (65 variants; 54 synonymous and 11 protein-changing).
Windows around 2.05-2.06 Mb retain strong protein-changing peaks, including
ABTJ_RS09755, ABTJ_RS09760 and ABTJ_RS09790.

`data/mutation_density_all.tsv` and
`data/mutation_density_nonsynonymous.tsv` are the annotated SNP inputs. The
10-kb peak and annotation tables are derived from those inputs.
