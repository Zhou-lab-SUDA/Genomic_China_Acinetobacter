# Revision source and reproducibility checks

Sources are the November 14 manuscript and supplementary information, the accompanying main and Extended Data figure PDFs, the submitted workbook, and the supporting analysis exports. Baseline public main is `1160ced006553fbfb4c0f8cd1ead7dd5d2b4f5fb`.

- **Figure numbering.** The submitted supplementary PDF and manuscript Methods assign dissemination adjustment to Supplementary Fig. 7. The reply refers to Fig. 6, which is the phenotype composite in the submitted PDF. Fig. 3d–f follows the submitted figure and figure legend.
- **Speed estimand and intervals.** The supplied annual series and fixed-clock model reproduce the percentage estimates and absolute-change CIs. The workflow starts from the historical analysis snapshot of annual km/SNP and sampling counts. A direct branch-event reconstruction of the manuscript's legacy speed summaries could not be verified from the available tree and pipeline. The relative-change limits quoted in the reply (−96.4% to 23.6% and −98.1% to −11.3%) differ from the archived positive-draw Monte Carlo intervals. Fig. 7 labels a row “Total”. Its placebo P = 0.843 matches the scaled ESL2.5-minus-ESL2.4 change contrast. This row requires label/estimand reconciliation before reuse as a pooled effect.
- **Network versions.** Fig. 3a–b retains a legacy network connectivity snapshot. The sampling sensitivity uses the Table S2 degree field and an overlapping but distinct cohort. Fig. 3e of the supplement (bloodstream) uses the earlier connectivity snapshot retained with that specimen analysis. The prevalence denominator in Fig. 3 and the major-lineage counts used for sampling depth are distinct inputs.
- **Nasal measurements.** The data match the submitted nasal raw table. The published panel legend joins mouse labels across days, while the assay Methods describe tissue collection following euthanasia at each time point. The plotting script uses independent observations per day and a line for the isolate mean. Source plate-level ANOVA reconstruction is outside this plot update.
- **Fig. 4l and Supplementary Fig. 6c.** Systemic-infection survival reproducibility is outside this public release. The existing research record remains separate.
- **Supplementary tables.** The submitted workbook and separately exported revision addition tables have different organisation. The S2/S9/S10 filenames in the new modules identify the addition export series and do not imply worksheet positions in the submitted workbook.
